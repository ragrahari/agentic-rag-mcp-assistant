# Corporate Card Program Guide

## Card types

Every employee approved for travel receives a virtual corporate card by default. Employees
who travel more than twice per quarter may request a physical card in addition to the
virtual card. Both card types draw from the same per-employee spend limit.

## Spend limits and merchant categories

Default monthly spend limits are set by department: $2,000 for individual contributors,
$4,000 for managers, and $8,000 for directors and above. Limits can be temporarily raised
by a finance administrator for a specific trip. Cards are restricted to travel-related
merchant categories (airlines, hotels, rail, ground transportation, and dining) by default;
other categories require an explicit administrator override.

## Disputing a transaction

If a cardholder does not recognize a transaction or believes it was charged in error, they
should flag it from the transactions list within 60 days of the transaction date. Flagged
transactions are placed in a "disputed" state and excluded from the cardholder's expense
report until the dispute is resolved. Disputes typically resolve within 5-7 business days.

## Lost or stolen cards

A lost or stolen card should be frozen immediately from the mobile app or by contacting
support. Freezing a card blocks new transactions but preserves transaction history. A
replacement virtual card can be issued instantly; a replacement physical card ships within
3-5 business days.
