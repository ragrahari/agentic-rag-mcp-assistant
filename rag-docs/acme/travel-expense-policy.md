# Travel & Expense Policy

## Per-diem limits

Employees traveling on approved business trips receive a daily meal per-diem. Domestic
travel within the continental United States is capped at $75 per day. International travel
is capped at $110 per day to account for higher costs of living in most international
business destinations. Per-diem amounts are not affected by the traveler's seniority level.

Any single meal exceeding $60 requires an itemized receipt, regardless of whether the total
daily per-diem cap was respected. Alcohol is reimbursable only when it appears on a client-
facing meal receipt and does not exceed 20% of that receipt's total.

## Hotel bookings that include a conference fee

When a hotel booking includes a bundled conference or event registration fee, the booking
must be split into two separate expense line items at submission time: the room-and-tax
portion should be categorized as "Lodging," and the bundled fee should be categorized as
"Conference & Training." Do not submit the combined charge as a single "Lodging" line — this
misclassifies the conference fee and will be flagged in expense review.

## Booking outside the approved platform

All flights, hotels, and rail travel must be booked through the approved corporate travel
platform. Bookings made outside the approved platform are reimbursable only if the approved
platform had no available option for the required route or dates, and the employee obtained
manager pre-approval in writing before booking. Out-of-platform bookings submitted without
pre-approval will be denied, even if the amount is otherwise within policy limits.

## Ground transportation

Rideshare and taxi expenses are reimbursable for trips to/from airports, client sites, and
between meeting locations during a business trip. Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at
the current IRS standard mileage rate.
