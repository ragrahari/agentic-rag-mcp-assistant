# Product Overview

Our platform is a business travel and corporate spend management product built for
finance and operations teams at mid-market and enterprise companies. It combines travel
booking, corporate card issuance, and expense management into a single system so that
travel spend is visible and policy-compliant from the moment a trip is booked, rather than
reconstructed after the fact from receipts.

## What the platform does

- **Travel booking:** employees book flights, hotels, and rail through a single approved
  marketplace, with company travel policy enforced automatically at the point of booking
  (e.g., fare class limits, preferred vendor discounts, advance-purchase requirements).
- **Corporate cards:** virtual and physical corporate cards are issued per employee, with
  spend limits and merchant category restrictions configured per department.
- **Expense management:** card transactions and manually submitted expenses flow into a
  single review queue, categorized automatically and checked against the travel and
  expense policy before manager approval.

## Who it's for

The platform is designed for finance teams who need real-time visibility into travel and
card spend, and for employees who want to book travel and get reimbursed without manual
paperwork. It is not designed for personal or consumer travel booking.

## Support

Finance administrators can reach platform support through the in-app help widget or the
admin support email. Standard response time for policy configuration questions is one
business day.
