# Reimbursement Process

## Submitting an out-of-pocket expense

Expenses paid out-of-pocket (not on a corporate card) are submitted as a manual expense
report entry with a photo or scan of the receipt attached. Reports should be submitted
within 30 days of the expense date. Reports submitted after 90 days require director-level
approval regardless of amount.

## Approval flow

Manual expense reports route to the submitter's direct manager for approval. Reports that
include any line item categorized as "Other" or that exceed $500 in total additionally
route to a finance reviewer before payment. Approved reports are queued for the next
payroll or accounts-payable disbursement cycle, whichever is configured for that employee's
country.

## Reimbursement timing

Once approved, reimbursements are disbursed within 2 payroll cycles for employees on
payroll-linked reimbursement, or within 5 business days for employees on direct
accounts-payable disbursement. Rejected reports are returned to the submitter with a reason
and can be corrected and resubmitted.
