# Corporate Card Program Guide

## Card types

Every employee approved for travel receives a virtual corporate card by default. Physical
cards are issued only on request from a department head, regardless of travel frequency.

## Spend limits and merchant categories

Default monthly spend limits are set by department: $1,500 for individual contributors,
$3,000 for managers, and $6,000 for directors and above. Limits can be temporarily raised
by a finance administrator for a specific trip. Cards are restricted to travel-related
merchant categories (airlines, hotels, rail, ground transportation, and dining) by default.

## Disputing a transaction

If a cardholder does not recognize a transaction or believes it was charged in error, they
should flag it from the transactions list within 45 days of the transaction date. Flagged
transactions are placed in a "disputed" state and excluded from the cardholder's expense
report until the dispute is resolved.

## Lost or stolen cards

A lost or stolen card should be frozen immediately from the mobile app or by contacting
support. A replacement virtual card can be issued instantly; a replacement physical card
ships within 7-10 business days.
