# Product Overview

Globex Corporation uses this platform to manage business travel and corporate card spend
for its distributed manufacturing and logistics workforce. Travel booking, card issuance,
and expense management are combined into a single system so that travel spend stays
policy-compliant from the moment a trip is booked.

## What the platform does for Globex

- **Travel booking:** employees book flights, hotels, and rail through a single approved
  marketplace, with Globex's travel policy enforced automatically at the point of booking.
- **Corporate cards:** virtual and physical corporate cards are issued per employee, with
  spend limits and merchant category restrictions configured per department.
- **Expense management:** card transactions and manually submitted expenses flow into a
  single review queue, categorized automatically and checked against Globex's travel and
  expense policy before manager approval.

## Support

Globex finance administrators can reach platform support through the in-app help widget.
Standard response time for policy configuration questions is one business day.
