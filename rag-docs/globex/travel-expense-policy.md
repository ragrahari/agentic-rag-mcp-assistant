# Travel & Expense Policy

## Per-diem limits

Employees traveling on approved business trips receive a daily meal per-diem. Domestic
travel within the continental United States is capped at $90 per day. International travel
is capped at $150 per day. Per-diem amounts are not affected by the traveler's seniority
level.

Any single meal exceeding $80 requires an itemized receipt, regardless of whether the total
daily per-diem cap was respected. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances.

## Hotel bookings that include a conference fee

When a hotel booking includes a bundled conference or event registration fee, the combined
charge may be submitted as a single "Lodging" line item as long as the total does not exceed
$200 per night. Combined charges above that threshold must be split into "Lodging" and
"Conference & Training" line items at submission time.

## Booking outside the approved platform

All flights, hotels, and rail travel must be booked through the approved corporate travel
platform. Out-of-platform bookings are reimbursable only with prior written manager
approval, and only when documented at the time of booking, not after the fact.

## Ground transportation

Rideshare and taxi expenses are reimbursable for trips to/from airports, client sites, and
between meeting locations during a business trip. Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at
the current IRS standard mileage rate.
